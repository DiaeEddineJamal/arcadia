# Arcadia Release Notes

## Version 1.0.0 - Initial Release 🎉

Welcome to Arcadia! Your personal ambient soundscape creator is now available.

### ✨ What's New

**🎵 Core Features**
- Mix unlimited ambient sounds simultaneously
- Individual volume controls for each sound
- Master volume adjustment
- Smooth, seamless sound looping
- Background playback support

**🌿 Rich Sound Library**
- **Rain**: Light rain, heavy rain, thunder
- **Ocean**: Rolling waves and beach ambience
- **Nature**: Forest sounds with birds and wildlife
- **Wind**: Gentle breezes through trees
- **Fire**: Crackling fireplace sounds
- **White Noise**: White, pink, and brown noise for focus
- **Fantasy**: Enchanted atmospheres (Pirates, Tavern, Tavern Singing)

**⏰ Sleep Timer**
- Set custom timer duration
- Automatic fade-out to help you fall asleep
- Gentle volume reduction

**🎨 Beautiful Design**
- Modern glassmorphism UI with stunning visual effects
- Animated video backgrounds
- Dark and light theme support
- Customizable accent colors
- Smooth animations optimized for 120Hz displays

**⚡ Performance Optimized**
- Optimized audio mixing engine
- Low RAM and CPU usage
- Efficient battery consumption
- Smooth 60+ FPS animations
- Maximum refresh rate support

**🔧 Technical Features**
- Completely offline - no internet required
- Background audio playback
- Hardware-accelerated video backgrounds
- Responsive volume sliders with instant feedback
- Smart audio service with lazy loading

### 🛠️ Technical Improvements

- **Memory Optimized**: Limited asset cache with LRU eviction (reduced from 3GB+ to 200-500MB)
- **Smooth Animations**: Optimized all transitions to 250-280ms with easeOutCubic curves
- **Scroll Performance**: Enhanced with ClampingScrollPhysics and increased cache extents
- **Audio Engine**: Lazy secondary player initialization, intelligent looping, throttled position monitoring
- **Video Background**: Hardware-accelerated playback with widget caching

### 📱 Platform Support

- ✅ Android
- ✅ iOS
- ✅ All screen sizes and refresh rates

### 🎯 Perfect For

- Focus & Productivity
- Sleep & Relaxation
- Meditation & Mindfulness
- Studying
- Tinnitus Relief
- Work Environment Masking
